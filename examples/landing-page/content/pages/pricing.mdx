Graft is open source and self-hostable — the **Solo** tier is free forever.
The **Team** tier adds managed Postgres branches and hosted MCP endpoints for
$29 per project per month, and **Fleet** is custom pricing for organizations
running agents at scale. Every tier includes the full CLI, the MCP server,
and git-native versioning; there are no per-seat charges, because most of
your operators aren't humans.
