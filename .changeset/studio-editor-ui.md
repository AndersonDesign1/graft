Studio is rebuilt for editors. Content comes first: a home page with what is waiting to publish and the collections as a table of contents, a table per collection searched, filtered, sorted and paged on the server with bulk publish, discard and delete, and an entry editor that shows the title in the site's display face, every field with its label, limits and help, and the content in the rich editor. Fields edit in place, including choices, money, references with a search picker, tags, image galleries, nested groups and repeatable lists such as product variants; "Edit in Raw MDX" is gone as the only way to change them. Problems are reported on the field in plain words before a save, and a change made elsewhere since the entry was opened is reported instead of overwritten. Publishing happens in one sheet that names what will happen (publish, submit for review, or commit), lists the changes as entries with their changed fields ("Price: $139.00 → $145.50"), resolves conflicts, and discards. New entries ask only for required fields. A hosted Studio shows a sign-in screen. Index health, schema, history, branches, approvals and settings move under Developer. `ContentTreePanel` now renders the full editor.
