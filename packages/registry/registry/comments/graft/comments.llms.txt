## comments (primitive)

Moderated comments backed by db-authoritative rows (`graft/comments.ts`).
Functions are served at POST /api/fn/<name>:

- `postComment` — public; `{ pageSlug, author, body }` → stores it unapproved. 5/min per caller.
- `listComments` — public; `{ pageSlug, limit? }` → approved comments, newest first.
- `moderateComment` — `{ id, approved }` → approve/hide. Needs the `content:moderate` scope.
- `deleteComment` — `{ id }` → destructive: files an approval (`graft approve <id>`), then
  retry with the `x-graft-approval` header; also needs `content:moderate`.

New comments are hidden until `moderateComment` approves them. Depends on the
`scoped-access` primitive (`graft/scoped-access.ts`) for the moderation gate.
