## commerce (primitive)

Catalog + orders foundation in `graft/commerce.ts`.

### Catalog (files)

Author products under `content/products/<slug>.mdx`:

    ---
    title: Widget
    description: A fine widget.
    priceCents: 2900
    currency: USD
    active: true
    ---

    Longer product body (MDX)…

Then `graft compile`. List via sdk `listContent("products")` or MCP `list_content`.

### Orders (Postgres)

Functions at POST /api/fn/<name>:

- `placeOrder` — public; `{ email, items: [{ productSlug, qty }] }` → creates a
  `pending` order with snapshotted unit prices. Unknown/inactive products 400.
- `listOrders` — needs `commerce:orders:read`; `{ limit? }`.
- `updateOrderStatus` — needs `commerce:orders:write`; `{ id, status }` where
  status is pending|paid|cancelled|fulfilled.
- `cancelOrder` — destructive hard-delete; needs write scope + `graft approve`
  then retry with `x-graft-approval`. Prefer `updateOrderStatus` → cancelled
  for soft cancel.

Mint scopes on your issuer (example: GRAFT_DEV_TOKEN or Better Auth payload).
